I bring nearly 30 years of experience across creative practice, curation, coaching, facilitation, learning design and organisational development. That breadth lets me notice connections and subtleties, listen from different perspectives, and shape an approach around the people, purpose and context.

Learning design

‘We want to create meaningful learning’

Meaningful learning begins with people, purpose and context. By recognising what people already bring, what they need and what you’re trying to achieve together, learning can be relevant, purposeful and able to evolve as those needs change.

I bring the structure, experience and breadth of perspective to help shape the right framework — while staying open to where the learning takes us. It also means creating an environment built on trust, where people feel at ease and do their best thinking.

Explore learning design →

Creative development & curation

‘We have an idea we want to bring to life’

Creative development and curation are about relationships: between an artist and their work, between individual works, between work and space, and between the artist and the people experiencing it. I approach it as a journey of exploration — noticing connections, asking questions, and considering how an idea can be communicated while letting the artist and the work keep their own voice.
